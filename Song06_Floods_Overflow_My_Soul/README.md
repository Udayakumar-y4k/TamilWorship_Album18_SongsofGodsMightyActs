# Floods Overflow My Soul - வெள்ளங்கள் என் ஆத்துமா மட்டும் பெருகிவருகிறது

Psalm 69 – David cries from overwhelming floods (v.1–2), deep mire (v.2), weary calling (v.3), enemies without cause (v.4), reproach for God’s sake (v.7–9), plea for rescue (v.13–15), hope in God’s salvation (v.29). Foreshadows Christ’s suffering (John 15:25; Romans 15:3).
Closing Prayer
Lord Jesus Christ, who endured floods for us,
thank You for hearing our cries in deep waters.
Lift us from mire, hide not Your face, deliver us.
In the eternal day, let us stand firm in Your salvation.
Come quickly, our Refuge in the flood.
