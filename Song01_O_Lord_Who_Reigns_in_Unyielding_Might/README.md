Celebrates God’s power: sea to dry land (Exodus 14:21–22), Jordan parted (Joshua 3:14–17), refining like silver (Psalm 66:10), leading through fire/water to abundance (Psalm 66:12). Points to Jesus’ authority over creation (Matthew 8:26–27 – storm calmed) and ultimate deliverance.
Closing Prayer
Lord Jesus Christ, Mighty Ruler over sea and land,
thank You for turning floods to paths and trials to triumph.
Lead us through fire and water, refine us like silver.
In the eternal day, let us praise Your mighty acts forever.
Come quickly, our Deliverer and King.
ஆமென். அல்லேலூயா!
