# When God Arises in This Earth - தேவன் இந்த பூமியில் எழுந்தருளும்போது

Psalm 68 – God arises, enemies scatter like smoke, melt like wax before fire. He is Father to orphans, defender of widows, gives home to lonely, leads captives to freedom. Righteous rejoice. Echoes Exodus 15:1–21 (God’s victory over enemies) and Isaiah 61:1 (liberty to captives).
Closing Prayer
Lord Jesus Christ, when You arise, enemies scatter.
Arise in power, scatter darkness, defend the fatherless and widow.
Set captives free, give home to the lonely.
In the eternal day, let us rejoice in Your victory forever.
Come quickly, our Rising King.
