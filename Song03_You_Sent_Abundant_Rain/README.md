# You Sent Abundant Rain - சம்பூரண மழையைப் பெய்யப்பண்ணினீர்

Psalm 68 – God sends abundant rain, refreshes weary land, provides for poor, scatters enemies, ascends on high, receives gifts. Echoes Exodus 14–15 (Red Sea), Joshua 3 (Jordan), Isaiah 55:10–11 (rain brings fruitfulness).
Closing Prayer
Lord Jesus Christ, Giver of abundant rain and life,
thank You for refreshing our weary souls.
Send Your Spirit like rain, sustain us in dryness.
In the eternal day, let us dwell in Your fruitful land.
Come quickly, our Provider.
