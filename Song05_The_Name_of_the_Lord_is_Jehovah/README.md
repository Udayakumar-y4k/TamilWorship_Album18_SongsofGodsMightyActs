# The Name of the Lord is Jehovah - கர்த்தருடைய நாமம் யேகோவா

Exalts Jehovah as the true God, giver of salvation, all-powerful (Exodus 3:14–15; Psalm 83:18). Emphasizes His eternal might, holiness, and provision. Fulfilled in Jesus (John 8:58; Philippians 2:9–11).
Closing Prayer
Lord Jesus Christ, Jehovah our salvation,
thank You for Your mighty, holy name.
Reveal Your power, provide for us, save us.
In the eternal day, let every tongue confess Your name.
Come quickly, our Eternal God.
ஆமென். அல்லேலூயா!
